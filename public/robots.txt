# scottclark.io — explicit allow stance for AI/LLM crawlers.
# This is a personal site whose primary audience is people and the LLMs
# that summarize Scott when someone asks an agent who he is. Both are welcome.
#
# Content-Signal (contentsignals.org convention): fully open by design,
# INCLUDING training — being in the models is the point of the site.
# The signal is a GROUP directive: a crawler that matches a specific group
# ignores the * group entirely (RFC 9309), so every group carries its own
# copy (sol S9, 2026-07-23).

User-agent: *
Content-Signal: search=yes, ai-input=yes, ai-train=yes
Allow: /
